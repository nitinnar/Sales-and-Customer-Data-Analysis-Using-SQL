# Sales-and-Customer-Data-Analysis-Using-SQL

In this project, I worked with a sales and customer dataset to perform a series of analyses aimed at enhancing business strategies. The project showcases my ability to clean and transform raw data into meaningful insights, a skill vital for any data-focused role. Key tasks included:

**Data Cleaning: **Removed duplicates, handled missing values, and filtered out invalid records to ensure data integrity.
Customer Segmentation: Segmenting customers into categories such as ‘Premium,’ ‘Standard,’ and ‘Basic’ based on total spending, which provides insights into customer behavior.

**Sales Trends:** Analyzing monthly and yearly sales trends, helping understand patterns in purchasing behavior across different time frames.
Payment Method Analysis: Determining the revenue contribution of various payment methods, allowing for targeted financial strategies.
Targeted Marketing: Identifying high-value customers for personalized marketing campaigns to increase customer retention and sales.
Revenue Breakdown: Understanding the total spending per customer and identifying areas of growth through focused analysis.
Category Spending: Analyzing spending trends by product category to identify areas for product or inventory optimization.
Invalid Data Removal: Filtering out negative sales values to maintain clean data and accurate reporting.
